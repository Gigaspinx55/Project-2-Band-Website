Background story

Skillet started at the Covenant Community Church in Memphis, Tennessee. John Cooper, Ken Steorts and Trey McClurkin all played at a praising band in this church, until their pastor suggested that they joined forces to make a new band, despite having divergent musical backgrounds. He told them to "toss a bunch of different things into a big ol' skillet and see how it turns out", which not only got the three of them to form their band, but also to name it Skillet.
